.. _per-chat-history:

==================================================
Chat history, sharing, and export
==================================================

Every conversation you have with Pér is kept. This article covers finding one again, keeping the
list usable, handing a chat to a colleague, and taking one out of Pér.

This is the :ref:`Learn <per-customer-decision-loop-learn>` stage of the customer decision loop, in its most literal form — nothing is thrown away.


.. _per-chat-history-finding:

Finding a chat again
==================================================

Your chats are the index of everything you have asked Pér. They are listed newest first, and the list is specific to your Pér usage.

* **Only your own chats are listed.** A colleague does not see them unless you share one.
* **Rename a chat** to something you will recognize. Until you do, it is named by your first
  message — see :ref:`What a conversation is <per-chatting-what-it-is>`.
* **Pin the ones you keep coming back to.** Pinned chats are grouped above the rest.
* **Search by anything said in a chat**, not just by its name.

The list also tells you what each chat is doing: which are still working, which have an answer
waiting that you have not read, and which had a response fail. That is the other half of work
running on the server — you can start something, go elsewhere, and find out from the list that it
finished.


.. _per-chat-history-deleting:

Deleting a chat
==================================================

Deleting a chat removes it for good, and takes not-yet-approved work with it.

* **Deleting cannot be undone.** Pér asks you to confirm, and names the chat it will remove.
* **Anything in it still waiting for your approval can no longer be run.** A change Pér proposed
  and you never approved goes away with the conversation, even if you have a tab still showing it. 
  See :ref:`What Pér can't do, whatever you approve <per-approvals-limits>`.

.. note::

   Deleting a chat does not delete what it produced. A report Pér wrote and a file you attached are
   kept as :ref:`artifacts <per-artifacts>` and survive the conversation.


.. _per-chat-history-sharing:

Sharing a chat with a colleague
==================================================

You can give a colleague a link that lets them read a whole chat and carry on in their own copy.

This is a handoff, not shared editing, and the distinction decides what each of you sees
afterwards. They get everything you established; you keep your thread exactly as it was.

How it works:

* **Reading, then forking.** Your colleague opens the link and reads the whole conversation. The
  moment they reply, they get their own copy of it and carry on there. Nothing they do reaches
  your chat.
* **They have to be in your shared tenant.** The link works for someone signed in to Pér with 
  access to the same tenant, and for nobody else. A link does not grant access to a tenant.
* **If they are signed in to a different tenant of yours**, Pér offers to switch them rather than
  telling them the link is broken.
* **One link per chat.** Asking to share the same chat again hands back the same link.
* **A chat with nothing in it cannot be shared.** There is nothing to hand over yet.
* **You can turn sharing off.** The link stops working for anyone who still has it.

.. important::

   Turning sharing off stops new copies only. A colleague who already started their own copy keeps
   it — their conversation is theirs now, and you cannot withdraw it.


.. _per-chat-history-export:

Taking a chat out of Pér
==================================================

A chat exports as Markdown, to the clipboard or as a file.

This is for the times the destination is not Pér: a ticket, a planning document, a mail you write
yourself, etc. The export is a fresh read of the saved conversation, so what you get is what is stored rather than what happens to be on screen.

.. note::

   A chat exports while it is settled, not while it is still answering. Wait for the response to
   finish and try again.

For a report rather than a transcript, export the artifact instead — see
:ref:`Taking one with you <per-artifacts-export>`.


.. _per-chat-history-using:

Working with your chats
==================================================

**To find an earlier chat**

#. Click **Search chats** above the chat list.
#. Type anything said in the chat, or part of its name.

**To rename a chat**

#. Open the chat's menu and choose **Rename**.
#. Type the new name and press Enter.

**To pin a chat**

#. Open the chat's menu and choose **Pin**.

Choose **Unpin** to move it back.

**To delete a chat**

#. Open the chat's menu and choose **Delete**.
#. Confirm.

**To share a chat with a colleague**

#. Open the chat and click **Share this chat**.
#. Click **Copy link** and send the link to them.

**To turn off a chat's share link**

#. Open the chat and click **Share this chat**.
#. Click **Turn off sharing**, then confirm.

**To export a chat**

#. Open the chat and click **Export chat**.
#. Choose **Copy Markdown** or **Download Markdown**.

Your conversations are listed in :ref:`the sidebar <per-interface-tour-sidebar>`.
