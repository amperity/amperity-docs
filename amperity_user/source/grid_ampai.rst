==================================================
AI Assistant
==================================================

.. ampai-about-start

The **AI Assistant** is the conversational interface to your customer data in Amperity. Describe what you want in plain language to ask questions about your customers, create segments, and build journeys. Tool-specific **AI Assistants** bring that same conversation into the segment, journey, query, and Amps consumption editors.

.. ampai-about-end
